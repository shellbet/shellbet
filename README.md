## Who We Are

**shellbet** is an independent security research team founded by two pentesters with combined hands-on experience across a wide attack surface — from web and mobile applications to local networks and emerging AI systems.

We operate at the intersection of offensive security and knowledge sharing. Our work feeds back into the ecosystem: every finding documented, every vulnerability disclosed, every technique written up as a public article.

## Goals

- **CVEs** — Discover and responsibly disclose novel vulnerabilities, building a track record of credited findings
- **Hall of Fame** — Earn recognition from major vendors through responsible disclosure programs
- **Research & Articles** — Publish technical write-ups that contribute to the offensive security community
- **Open Tools** — Release tooling and PoCs that support reproducibility and peer verification
